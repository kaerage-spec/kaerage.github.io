My University Work & Projects

Project 1: Multi Dimensional Clustering and Visual Analytics

Newcastle University

In this project, I engineered a quantitative data analysis model in Excel to profile and map 30 distinct chemical compounds, defining performance thresholds based on intersecting variables. By plotting multi axis datasets such as cLogP versus pKa and Molecular Surface Area, I created visual segmentation models and drew precise activity contours to separate active metrics from inactive outliers. This exercise allowed me to discover clear trend boundaries and isolate specific data clusters. From an analyst perspective, this directly mirrors market segmentation and risk profiling, demonstrating a practical ability to clean complex datasets and deliver structured data visualisations to guide strategic decisions.

Project 2: Experimental Formulation Optimisation and Predictive Stability Study

Newcastle University

This project involved an evaluative research study to resolve generic formulation defects by prototyping a hypothesised, alcohol free chemical solution. I systematically tracked and isolated cosolvent concentrations against a constant variable of Phenobarbital at 400mg per mL to discover optimal stability thresholds, utilising the Arrhenius Accelerated Stability Test framework to mathematically model chemical degradation kinetics. This research successfully verified an alternative formulation method that forecast a stable product shelf life exceeding 24 months. This practical exercise in quality control and process optimisation proves an ability to investigate underlying operational defects, audit data against rigid compliance guidelines, and build predictive lifecycle models.

Project 3: Macro Trend Mapping and Quantitative Data Modelling

Newcastle University

For this project, I cleansed and synthesised large scale epidemiological datasets from global repositories including the CDC and WHO to evaluate mortality baselines and macro economic cost implications of 55 billion dollars per year. To support this research, I processed complex interaction datasets, calculated metrics using mathematical formulas, and engineered curves to apply basic statistical methods to test for differences in effects. This allowed me to map global trend variations and identify direct correlations connecting systemic process gaps to high financial waste. This combines macro trend evaluation with cost benefit analysis, proving an ability to extract information from global repositories and synthesise complex findings cleanly for stakeholders.
