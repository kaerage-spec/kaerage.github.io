Shifting My Mindset: Root-Cause Analysis & Systems Thinking

An exploration of business logic, process architecture, and structured problem-solving.

Business analysis is more than documenting requests—it is a systematic method of isolating operational inefficiencies, identifying process risks, and translating complex system rules into structured documentation to support strategic decision-making.

Coming from a high-level mathematical background, my approach is rooted in data integrity, process mapping, and meticulous attention to detail. Whether manually auditing hundreds of physical medication records at Freeman Hospital to enforce procedural compliance, or reconciling transaction streams at Fountain Pharmacy to isolate system discrepancies, I focus on establishing clean operational baselines.

True analysis relies on cutting through messy information to reveal objective facts that optimise workflows, reduce corporate liabilities, and save organisations time and cost.

Featured Business Analysis Projects

📋 Project 1: Functional Requirement Categorisation & Logic Scoping

Managing structural parameters and grouping ambiguous variables.
  • The Business Challenge: Translating 30 highly complex, multi-variable component profiles into strict, non-conflicting operational rules to guide technical implementation teams and senior stakeholders.
  • The BA Approach: Analysed core operational parameters and grouped ambiguous variables into a structured logic matrix based on precise numerical thresholds (cLogP vs. pKa metrics). Formatted clear categorical classification legends to eliminate analytical noise.
  • Business Impact: Developed an interactive, visual decision-support model that allows business teams to instantly cross-verify structural components against safety and regulatory requirements.

⚠️ Project 2: System Risk Appraisal & Regulatory Optimisation

Balancing regulatory frameworks against commercial constraints.
  • The Business Challenge: Identifying structural liabilities in existing product formulation and distribution streams to minimise demographic user risk while maintaining absolute regulatory alignment.
  • The BA Approach: Conducted a comprehensive root-cause analysis on variable interindividual clearance data streams (17.3mg/dL/h) and toxic load accumulation limits across narrow therapeutic index (NTI) margins. Balanced complex trade-offs between commercial viability and safety constraints to meet strict British Pharmacopoeia regulatory frameworks.
  • Business Impact: Delivered a strategic optimisation blueprint and predictive stability model forecasting a secure 24-month operational baseline, effectively eliminating process vulnerabilities and lowering systemic organisational risk.

🦠 Project 3: Macro-Process Mapping & Public Sector Impact Analysis

Aggregating fragmented operational data into business intelligence briefs.
  • The Business Challenge: Aggregating fragmented international operational reports to isolate the core behavioural and financial drivers behind an escalating global public health crisis.
  • The BA Approach: Cleansed and standardised disparate international data metrics sourced from the CDC, WHO, and European Commission. Mapped financial impacts ($55B/year operational leakages) and severe process bottlenecks (hospital stay durations expanding from 6.4 to 12.7 average days) to isolate systemic inefficiencies.
  • Business Impact: Produced a structured 1,500-word business intelligence brief isolating consumer non-compliance patterns to guide strategic regional resource allocation and support antimicrobial stewardship programmes.
