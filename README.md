# kaerage.github.io
**Healthcare Data and Business Analysis Portfolio**

This repository contains data analysis and quality control projects focusing on process auditing, trend tracking, and information reporting within the healthcare sector.

Project 1: Quantitative Parameter Modelling and Scatter Analysis

Problem: Evaluating multi variable datasets across 30 target compounds to isolate specific property thresholds and map optimal areas of activity.
Tool: Data Modelling and Visualisation Software

Action:

Managed a dataset tracking chemical properties across 30 distinct compound records to isolate key comparative metrics.
Generated scatter visualisation models to map relationship trends between independent numerical variables.
Conducted cluster analysis by identifying and outlining boundary regions of high performance versus zero activity.
Cross referenced multi parameter constraints to isolate optimal distribution segments across multiple test cases.

Result: Transformed complex multi variable data points into a clear predictive scatter map that isolates exact performance boundaries for strategic decision support.

Project 2: Clinical Formulation Review and Risk Assessment

Problem: Evaluating clinical safety data to identify risks associated with chemical excipients in generic paediatric medications and proposing an alternative, safer formulation design.
Tool: Document Review and Data Collation

Action:

Audited clinical case studies and medical literature to isolate high risk variables within standard Phenobarbital formulations.
Collected raw data tracking ethanol elimination kinetics and clearance metrics across vulnerable patient demographics.
Conducted a comparative analysis between current manufacturing standards and alternative cosolvent systems.
Investigated stability metrics using accelerated test models to verify the commercial viability of an alternative propylene glycol formulation.

Result: Delivered a comprehensive risk appraisal and technical proposal detailing a more optimal stable, alcohol free formulation to improve patient safety outcomes.

Project 3: Global Trend Analysis and Risk Mapping

Problem: Evaluating the escalation and operational impact of antimicrobial resistance metrics across diverse geographic regions to highlight economic and public health strains.
Tool: Document Review and Trend Evaluation

Action:

Assisted with the audit of global health datasets to extract and model metrics regarding drug resistant bacterial strains.
Conducted regional comparative studies to track disparities in clinical resource utilisation and public education effectiveness.
Analysed operational hospital data to isolate drivers of medication misuse including patient compliance and demand metrics.
Quantified the economic burden of prolonged clinical stays by evaluating direct hospital expenditures and cost variances.

Result: Produced a comprehensive trend analysis highlighting systemic risks and resource allocation recommendations to help mitigate escalating hospital costs.
